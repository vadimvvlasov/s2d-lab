# Recommendation policy

## What "best" means

S2D may recommend the best-supported option only relative to explicit criteria and constraints. It must not claim universal optimality.

## Criteria provenance

Every important criterion must be labelled:
- PARTICIPANT_STATED
- INFERRED_FROM_CONVERSATION
- RESEARCHER_PROPOSED
- UNKNOWN

A personal rule from one participant does not become a group rule unless the group explicitly adopts it.

## Option comparison

Compare 2–4 distinct, feasible choices. Include only dimensions relevant to the question:
- fit to goal;
- evidence strength/directness;
- practical feasibility;
- benefits;
- trade-offs/risks;
- missing information.

Do not invent numerical weights. If criteria conflict or are incomplete, present conditional recommendations (e.g. Option A if speed matters; Option B if robustness matters).

## Recommendation wording

Use "Best-supported option under the stated/inferred criteria". Explain why it wins, its main compromise, when another option wins, and what evidence could change the conclusion.

## Financial and trading decisions

- Do not produce unsolicited buy/sell/short signals or new executable prices/stops/targets.
- If the participant asks to assess a trade plan, analyze the process and arithmetic while clearly stating uncertainty and missing inputs.
- Researcher-generated levels are hypotheses, not recommendations; label them explicitly and do not make them actionable.
- The decision owner remains the participant, not the model or the group.
