# Investopedia research skill

Use when the conversation contains ambiguous financial terminology or the user needs a concise explanation of a market/investing concept.

## Appropriate use
- Definitions and distinctions: yield vs return, margin vs leverage, ETF mechanics, order types, futures terminology, risk metrics, and similar concepts.
- Educational context to clarify what a term means.
- Identify terminology that may have been used imprecisely in the conversation.

## Limits
- Investopedia is an educational secondary source, not primary evidence for current prices, issuer-specific fees, historical performance, legal requirements, or strategy profitability.
- For product-specific or current facts, seek issuer, exchange, broker, regulator, or original-data sources.
- Do not infer that a strategy works because a concept is explained.
- Do not use the site as a substitute for formal quantitative validation.

## Access
Use genuine search/browsing tools when available. Do not assume an Investopedia API exists. Do not bypass access controls or use unauthorized scraping. If a page cannot be accessed, disclose that and use an appropriate alternative source.

## Output
State the concept in plain language, distinguish it from commonly confused concepts, and cite the specific page actually consulted. Omit the source if it adds no material value.
