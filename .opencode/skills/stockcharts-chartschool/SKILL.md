# StockCharts ChartSchool research skill

Use when the conversation discusses technical analysis, indicators, chart patterns, trend/momentum, market breadth, or StockCharts methodology.

Primary reference: https://chartschool.stockcharts.com/

## Appropriate use
- Understand indicator definitions, formulas, parameters, and intended interpretation.
- Clarify how a technical method is constructed.
- Compare closely related indicators or identify parameter assumptions.
- Find the original ChartSchool explanation relevant to a claim in the conversation.

## Limits
- ChartSchool explains technical-analysis methods; it does not establish that a signal is profitable or robust.
- Do not turn a descriptive indicator rule into a trading recommendation.
- For performance claims, require reproducible historical testing with costs, realistic execution, and out-of-sample validation.
- Distinguish indicator calculation from strategy rules and from empirical profitability.

## Access and compliance
Use ordinary search and permitted page access only when actual tools are available. Do not scrape, crawl, bulk-download, or bypass technical/access controls. Respect the site's terms and robots/access restrictions. If automated access is disallowed or uncertain, rely on search snippets only for discovery and seek permitted official pages or alternative sources; do not claim to have read inaccessible content.

## Output
Give the exact concept/page, a concise explanation of what the source says, and the limitation relevant to the user's claim. Cite the specific URL actually consulted.
