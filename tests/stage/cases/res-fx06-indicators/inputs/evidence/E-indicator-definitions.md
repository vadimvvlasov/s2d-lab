# Indicator definitions

> SYNTHETIC TEST DATA. Frozen evidence pack for a T1 stage test. Not real market data, not a real quotation.

- Source: reference article on momentum oscillators, HTTP 200, full text opened, 2026-11-08
- RSI: 0–100 scale; above 70 conventionally overbought, below 30 oversold. Readings can stay extreme in strong trends; an extreme reading is not by itself a buy or sell signal.
- Stochastic oscillator: above 80 overbought, below 20 oversold.
- Bollinger %B: position of price within the bands; below 0 means price is under the lower band, near 0 means near the lower band.
