- RSI: momentum oscillator on a 0–100 scale. Readings above 70 are conventionally called overbought and below 30 oversold. An overbought reading is not by itself a sell signal; in strong trends RSI can stay above 70 for long periods.
- MACD: difference between two exponential moving averages, compared with a signal line.
- Bollinger Bands: moving average with bands set a number of standard deviations away.
