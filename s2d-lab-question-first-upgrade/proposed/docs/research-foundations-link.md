See `research/FOUNDATIONS.md` in the handoff package for the research rationale:
- QMSum — query-based meeting summarization.
- ExplainMeetSum — evidence-linked summaries.
- ARES — context relevance, answer faithfulness, answer relevance.
- SummEval — human dimensions of summary quality.
- QuestEval — QA-based factual coverage evaluation.

These are design patterns for the MVP, not instructions to deploy complex evaluation infrastructure.
