# Investopedia research skill

Use only when a participant's question depends on understanding a financial term or basic market mechanism.

Appropriate:
- definitions and plain-language explanations;
- distinctions such as dividend yield vs total return, margin vs leverage, ETF vs ETN, limit vs stop order;
- introductory context before a deeper primary-source check.

Limits:
- educational secondary source only;
- not primary evidence for current prices, issuer-specific fees/holdings, regulation, historical performance, or strategy profitability;
- verify important definitions against regulators, exchanges, issuers, or authoritative references where available;
- do not assume an API exists;
- use genuine permitted browser/search tools only; no unauthorized scraping or access bypass;
- cite only pages actually consulted.

Do not use Investopedia when a primary source answers the question more accurately without losing useful context.
