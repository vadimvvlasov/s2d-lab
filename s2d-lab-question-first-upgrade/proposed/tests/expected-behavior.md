8. For the market-bottom question, explain no indicator identifies a bottom with certainty; propose comparing pre-defined, testable rules rather than giving a forecast.
9. Cite only facts actually checked; never invent current holdings or IPO dates.
10. Produce an engaging recap a non-specialist can read in about 3–5 minutes.
11. Explicitly state no group decision or agreed action was made.
